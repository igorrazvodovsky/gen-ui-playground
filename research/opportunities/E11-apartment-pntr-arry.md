# E11 — Apartment comparison with PNTR/ARRY

Take the apartment comparison task. Model it with PNTR (apartment → neighbourhood, apartment → landlord) and ARRY (apartment → photos). Verify the json-render output can express the relationships (e.g. clicking an apartment shows its neighbourhood detail).

## What it validates

Whether JELLY's SVAL/DICT/PNTR/ARRY type system is expressive enough for relational data in the generative UI context.
