# E22 — Quality gates against LLM-generated specs

Apply quality gates (coverage, coherence, sufficiency) to LLM-generated specs. Measure: how often does generation pass on first attempt? What are the common failure modes? Does iterative regeneration (LLM fixes its own failures) converge?

## What it validates

Whether quality gates are a practical mechanism for improving generation reliability without human review of every output.
