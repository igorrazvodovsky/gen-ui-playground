The LLM queries a structured knowledge store (product schemas, business rules, vocabulary mappings) to decompose intent into operations. The knowledge store is the domain expertise the LLM lacks.

Fareedi et al. show 15% accuracy improvement with grounding. Also scoped as [[Knowledge-grounded task decomposition|V1]] in the vision tree — shared infrastructure for both operation and generation.
