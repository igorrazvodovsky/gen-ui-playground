The agent queries a structured knowledge store (product schemas, business rules, vocabulary) to decompose intent into valid operations. Recognised as shared infrastructure that serves both Branch 1 (operation) and Branch 2 (generation) — see [[Knowledge-grounded generation|S17]] for the generative UI variant.

## Riskiest assumption

A minimal knowledge store (schemas + rules + glossary, short of a full graph) is sufficient for reliable decomposition.
