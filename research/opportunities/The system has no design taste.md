Given an entity (a date, a list of items, a hierarchical dataset), there are many valid UI representations. A date could be a text input, a date picker, or a calendar. A list could be a table, cards, or a kanban board. Without design knowledge encoded somewhere, the system either makes arbitrary choices or always picks the default.
